The Four-Week Curriculum

Every week pairs a real HR use case with a practical AI application — so each session ends with something you can use the same day.

AI-Powered Talent Journey: From Hire to Onboard
Talent Acquisition & Employee Onboarding

The first week focuses on how AI and GPTs can support HR professionals throughout the talent acquisition and employee onboarding journey — making traditional recruitment activities faster, more structured and more effective.

1. AI-Powered Job Description Development

Using GPTs to create structured, professional Job Descriptions based on the requirements of a position.

  • Developing Job Descriptions using GPTs
  • Creating clear roles and responsibilities
  • Identifying skills, qualifications and experience requirements
  • Developing competency requirements for different positions
  • Improving existing Job Descriptions using AI
  • Creating role-specific recruitment content

Includes how the quality of your prompt and input information affects the quality of AI-generated outputs.

2. AI in Talent Acquisition

Practical applications of AI across different stages of recruitment:

  • Using GPTs for recruitment planning
  • Creating recruitment advertisements and job postings
  • Developing candidate sourcing approaches
  • Creating professional candidate communication
  • Developing interview questions based on the Job Description
  • Creating structured interview evaluation criteria
  • Preparing candidate assessment frameworks

The objective: AI as a recruitment assistant, not just a content-generation tool.

3. Resume Screening & Candidate Shortlisting

How GPTs can assist in reviewing candidate profiles against defined job requirements:

  • Comparing resumes against Job Descriptions
  • Identifying relevant skills and experience
  • Candidate screening based on predefined criteria
  • Comparing multiple candidate profiles
  • Creating structured candidate evaluation summaries
  • Supporting candidate shortlisting decisions

Includes a discussion on human review, fairness and responsible AI usage in recruitment decisions.

4. AI-Powered Employee Onboarding

Moving from hiring to onboarding:

  • Creating structured onboarding plans using AI
  • Developing joining and welcome communications
  • Creating employee induction schedules
  • Developing role-specific onboarding checklists
  • Creating FAQs for new employees
  • Using GPTs as onboarding knowledge assistants
  • Personalising the onboarding experience using AI
WEEK 1 OUTCOME

Use GPTs and AI tools to support the journey from Job Description → Recruitment → Candidate Screening → Selection Support → Employee Onboarding.

AI-Powered People Experience: Engage & Perform
Employee Engagement & Performance Management

Week 2 covers how employees experience the organisation, and how performance is managed and developed — moving from administrative processes towards more meaningful, data-supported people practices.

1. AI in Employee Engagement

Supporting the design, analysis and follow-up of engagement initiatives:

  • Creating Employee Engagement Surveys using GPTs
  • Developing organisation-specific survey questions
  • Creating pulse surveys
  • Analysing employee feedback
  • Identifying recurring themes in employee responses
  • Using AI for basic sentiment analysis
  • Identifying engagement drivers and areas of concern
  • Developing engagement action plans based on findings

2. AI for Employee Communication & Experience

How GPTs can support HR communication and employee experience:

  • Creating employee communications
  • Developing recognition and appreciation messages
  • Creating engagement campaign ideas
  • Generating employee FAQs
  • Personalising HR communication
  • Developing employee listening initiatives

Focus: quality, consistency and speed of HR communication — without losing the human connection.

3. AI-Powered Performance Management

How AI can support different stages of performance management:

  • Developing KPIs using GPTs
  • Aligning KPIs with roles and responsibilities
  • Developing measurable performance indicators
  • Supporting performance review preparation
  • Structuring performance review discussions
  • Analysing performance information
  • Creating performance summaries using AI
  • Identifying potential development areas

4. Employee Development through AI

Supporting employee development following a performance review:

  • Creating Individual Development Plans (IDPs)
  • Identifying development priorities
  • Developing learning recommendations
  • Creating Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs)
  • Structuring follow-up actions
  • Using AI to support continuous performance conversations
WEEK 2 OUTCOME

Use AI to strengthen the journey from Employee Engagement → Employee Feedback → Performance Measurement → Performance Review → Employee Development.

AI-Powered HR Governance: Policy to Compliance
HR Policies & New Labour Codes

Week 3 focuses on one of HR's most important responsibilities — developing clear HR policies, and helping professionals navigate complex employment and compliance-related information.

1. Developing HR Policies Using GPTs

How GPTs can assist in developing structured HR policies:

  • Developing HR policy frameworks
  • Drafting new HR policies using GPTs
  • Reviewing existing HR policies
  • Identifying gaps in policy documents
  • Simplifying complex policy language
  • Creating employee-friendly policy summaries
  • Developing FAQs based on HR policies
  • Creating policy communication materials

2. AI as an HR Policy Knowledge Assistant

Turning organisational HR policies into accessible knowledge resources:

  • Policy Q&A assistants
  • Employee FAQ assistants
  • HR knowledge assistants
  • Policy interpretation support
  • Quick retrieval of relevant policy information

How AI can help HR teams reduce repetitive queries while improving employee access to information.

3. Understanding the New Labour Codes with AI Support

Using AI to work with India's New Labour Codes:

  • Understand and simplify labour-law-related information
  • Summarise complex provisions for easier learning
  • Compare requirements with existing HR practices
  • Develop compliance checklists
  • Create internal awareness and communication materials
  • Organise labour-law knowledge for HR reference

4. Responsible AI in Policy & Compliance

Understanding the limitations of AI in sensitive areas such as policy interpretation and legal compliance.

AI-generated information should support professional work — critical policy and legal decisions require appropriate verification and expert review.

WEEK 3 OUTCOME

Use AI as a support system for HR Policy Development → Policy Review → Employee Communication → Labour Code Learning → Compliance Support.

Predict & Build: HR Analytics to AI Agents
Attrition Analysis & AI Agent Development

The final week moves from using AI tools to analysing HR information — and begins building AI-powered HR solutions. This is the culmination of the four-week journey.

1. Understanding Attrition through AI

Supporting employee attrition and retention analysis:

  • Understanding employee attrition data
  • Structuring HR data for analysis
  • Identifying attrition patterns and trends
  • Analysing potential reasons for employee turnover
  • Exploring department, tenure, role or other relevant patterns
  • Identifying possible retention concerns
  • Creating management-friendly attrition summaries

2. Moving from Attrition Data to Retention Insights

Understanding what the data may be telling HR — not just calculating attrition:

  • Identifying potential attrition drivers
  • Analysing employee feedback alongside attrition information
  • Creating retention-focused insights
  • Developing possible HR interventions
  • Preparing attrition dashboards and management summaries
  • Using AI to support evidence-based HR discussions

3. Introduction to AI Agents for HR

The next stage of AI adoption — AI Agents:

  • What is an AI Agent?
  • How is an AI Agent different from a normal chatbot or GPT?
  • Where can AI Agents be used in HR?
  • Identifying repetitive HR processes suitable for AI support
  • How instructions, knowledge and workflows work together
  • Understanding the basic architecture of an HR AI Agent

4. Developing Practical AI Agents for HR

Potential AI-agent applications across the HR lifecycle:

  • Recruitment Assistant
  • Resume Screening Assistant
  • Employee Onboarding Assistant
  • Performance Management Assistant
  • Employee Engagement Analysis Assistant
  • HR Policy Assistant
  • HR Knowledge Assistant
  • Attrition Analysis Assistant

The process: HR Problem → AI Use Case → Instructions → Knowledge → Workflow → AI Agent.

5. Becoming an AI-Ready HR Professional

How to continue the AI journey after the program — three levels:

  • Level 1 — Use AI effectively
  • Level 2 — Apply AI independently to HR problems
  • Level 3 — Build AI-powered solutions for HR
WEEK 4 OUTCOME

Understand how AI can support Attrition Analysis → Retention Insights → HR Decision Support → AI Agent Development.
